# Renaming Log — R8 2011-2020 Harmonized Labels

Every rename applied in the cleaned series, grouped by category. In each group all variants are the same NAICS category — verified by matching NAICS codes across vintages — so the rename changes presentation only, never meaning. The standardization rule: adopt the label BLS itself uses in the most recent vintages (2019-2020), on the argument that it is the current official nomenclature, it matches what any future-year additions will arrive with, and modern terminology supersedes outdated wording.

## Group 1 — Benchmark row (no NAICS)

| Label in source | Years |
|---|---|
| Private industry (all industries) | 2011-2015 (as built) |
| All Industry5,7 / All Industry(4)(5) / All Industry[4],[5] | 2016-2020 |

Standardized to: **Private industry (all industries)**.
Argument: the 2016-2020 label carries footnote markers baked into the text (three different formats), and "All Industry" overstates scope — the survey covers private industry only (state/local government are published separately). "Private industry" is the accurate BLS term and matches the PDF vintages.

## Group 2 — NAICS 621111

| Label in source | Years |
|---|---|
| Offices of physicians (except mental health specialists) | 2011-2015, 2019-2020 |
| Offices of physicians except mental health | 2016, 2018 |

Standardized to: **Offices of physicians (except mental health specialists)**.
Argument: seven of nine publishing vintages use the full parenthetical form; the 2016/2018 short form is a truncation of the same official title.

## Group 3 — NAICS 621112

| Label in source | Years |
|---|---|
| Offices of physicians, mental health specialists | 2011-2012, 2014-2015, 2019-2020 |
| Offices of mental health physicians | 2016-2018 |

Standardized to: **Offices of physicians, mental health specialists**.
Argument: majority form and current official form; the 2016-2018 wording is a paraphrase that inverts the NAICS title structure.

## Group 4 — NAICS 6223

| Label in source | Years |
|---|---|
| Specialty (except psychiatric and substance abuse) hospitals | 2011-2015, 2019-2020 |
| Other hospitals | 2016-2018 |

Standardized to: **Specialty (except psychiatric and substance abuse) hospitals**.
Argument: "Other hospitals" is ambiguous in a chart legend and collides conceptually with "Other residential care facilities" / "Other ambulatory health care services". The full title says what the category actually is and is the official NAICS 6223 title.

## Group 5 — NAICS 6231

| Label in source | Years |
|---|---|
| Nursing care facilities | 2011-2013, 2016-2018 |
| Nursing care facilities (skilled nursing facilities) | 2014-2015, 2019-2020 |

Standardized to: **Nursing care facilities (skilled nursing facilities)**.
Argument: current official title (NAICS 2012 onward added the SNF parenthetical); the parenthetical disambiguates from the 623 parent "Nursing and residential care facilities" in visuals.

## Group 6 — NAICS 6232

| Label in source | Years |
|---|---|
| Residential mental retardation, mental health and substance abuse facilities | 2011-2013 |
| Residential mental health facilities | 2016-2018 |
| Residential intellectual and developmental disability, mental health, and substance abuse facilities | 2014-2015, 2019-2020 |

Standardized to: **Residential intellectual and developmental disability, mental health, and substance abuse facilities**.
Argument: current official title. The 2011-2013 wording uses terminology that federal agencies formally retired (Rosa's Law, 2010) — carrying it forward in a public-facing portfolio would be both dated and avoidably jarring. The 2016-2018 short form drops two-thirds of the category's actual scope (IDD and substance abuse facilities), which misrepresents what the number covers.

## Group 7 — NAICS 6233

| Label in source | Years |
|---|---|
| Community care facilities for the elderly | 2011-2013, 2016-2018 |
| Continuing care retirement communities and assisted living facilities for the elderly | 2014-2015, 2019-2020 |

Standardized to: **Continuing care retirement communities and assisted living facilities for the elderly**.
Argument: current official title, and it names the two actual facility types (CCRCs and assisted living), which reads more concretely to a healthcare audience than the older generic label. Same NAICS code (6233) in every vintage, so this is purely a title revision by BLS, not a definitional change.

## Renames recorded but NOT applied (excluded social assistance branch)

For the record, if NAICS 624 is ever added: "Services for the elderly and disabled" (2016-2018) = "Services for the elderly and persons with disabilities" (62412, all other years); and in 2016-2018 the NAICS 6242 parent "Community food and housing, and emergency and other relief services" is mislabeled as "Emergency and other relief services" — it must be re-labeled to the full parent title or it collides with its own 62423 child of the same name.
